The Smarter Web Company PLC
(“The Smarter Web Company” or the "Company")
Employee Update
The Smarter Web Company (LSE: SWC | OTCQB: TSWCF | FRA: 3M8) announces that Jesse
Myers is leaving the Company with effect from 1 September 2026. The Company's Bitcoin
Treasury Policy remains unchanged and will continue to be overseen by the Board.
Andrew Webley, CEO, The Smarter Web Company, commented:
“I would like to thank Jesse for the contribution he has made to Smarter Web. We wish him
every success for the future. We remain fully focused on executing our strategy and delivering
our ambitions.”
