22 July 2026
The Smarter Web Company PLC
(“The Smarter Web Company” or the “Company”)
Capital Reduction Effective
The Smarter Web Company (LSE: SWC | OTCQB: TSWCF | FRA: 3M8) announces that, further
to the announcements made by the Company on 1 June 2026, 17 June 2026 and 15 July 2026,
the reduction by the Company of its share premium account by £210,000,000 (the "Capital
Reduction") is now effective. This follows the delivery of the Court order and accompanying
statement of capital to the Registrar of Companies on 15 July 2026, and the registration of
those documents by the Registrar of Companies today. The Capital Reduction therefore
became effective today.
Further details of the Capital Reduction are included in the Company’s circular dated 1 June
2026 (the "Circular"), which can be found on the Company's website
at: https://www.smarterwebcompany.co.uk/.
Terms used but not defined in this
announcement have the meaning given to them in the Circular.
