## Key Topics:
- Jesse Myers' (Croesus_BTC) interview echoes Vostra's own mNAV thesis discussed days earlier
- mNAV as the market's willingness to "overpay" in BTC terms for future BTC-per-share growth
- Bitcoin treasury companies described as "capital pumps" channeling fiat capital into BTC
- Comparing PE ratios (e.g. Apple at 35x) to "days to cover" mNAV for treasury companies
- Smarter Web Co. cited as having one of the fastest mNAV payback periods (~35 days)
- Breaking down valuation multiple into components: self-custody convenience, tax benefits, execution/management, clean cap table, investor support, no-discount capital plan, and market sentiment
- Discussion of how mNAV premiums typically compress over time as a treasury strategy matures
- Argument that consistently "delivering" on BTC-per-share growth justifies sustained premiums rather than being a bubble
