## Key Topics:
- Andrew Webley's background at Hargreaves Lansdown and founding of his web agency
- Reverse takeover of a dormant shell company to gain a stock market listing
- Early backing from Tyler Evans/UTXO Management and David Bailey

- Warrant buybacks, Coinbase borrowing, and selling Bitcoin to retire a convertible
