## Key Topics:
- Why Europe's Bitcoin treasury market (UK, Capital B, H100) is much smaller than the US market, driven by capital markets structure and investor risk profiles
- Bitcoin-per-share as the primary metric vs. total Bitcoin held as a secondary "optionality" metric
- Trading below 1x mNAV and the tools/strategies used to keep growing Bitcoin-per-share in a discounted environment
- The case for a "well-run Bitcoin treasury company is better than Bitcoin" (compounding argument, Yellow Cake uranium analogy)
- Why STRC/SATA digital credit instruments are ~80% retail-held and structural reasons institutions can't yet hold them
- Coinbase facility rate cut to 6%, what it signals about counterparty trust, and use of debt to buy back pre-IPO warrants
- Net vs. gross sats-per-share and how leverage amplifies shareholder value as Bitcoin appreciates
- Why no single metric (Bitcoin yield, mNAV, EV/BTC) tells the full story, and the move toward standardized real-time treasury analytics
- The relationship between Smarter Web's operating business (web design, Squarebird acquisition) and its Bitcoin treasury strategy
- Commitment to shareholder transparency via weekly updates, RNS disclosures, and real-time balance sheet data
