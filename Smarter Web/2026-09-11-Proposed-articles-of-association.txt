ALLOTMENT

5.1

Subject to these Articles, Company Legislation and the rights attached to any existing shares, the Board may
offer, allot, grant options over or otherwise dispose of shares in the Company, or offer, allot or issue instruments
or securities with the right to convert to shares in the Company, in each case to such persons and on such terms as
it may decide.

5.2

Subject to any rights attached to any existing shares, any share may be issued with or have attached to it such
rights or restrictions as the Company may by ordinary resolution decide (provided that such resolution is passed
before the allotment of the relevant share(s)) or, so far as the resolution does not make specific provision or if no
such resolution has been passed, as the Board may decide.

5.3

Subject to Company Legislation, any share may be issued which is to be redeemed, or is to be liable to be
redeemed at the option of the Company or the Holder or both, on such terms and conditions and in such manner
as the Board may decide.

5.4

The Company may exercise all powers conferred by Company Legislation of paying commissions and/or
brokerage in relation to a subscription for shares or other allotment. These commissions and/or brokerage may
be satisfied in cash or by the allotment of fully or partly paid shares or partly in one way and partly in the other.
CLASS RIGHTS

6

VARIATION

6.1

Whenever the share capital of the Company is divided into different classes, the rights attached to any class of
shares may only be varied with the consent in writing of the Holders of not less than three-quarters in nominal
value of the issued shares of the class or with the sanction of a special resolution passed at a separate meeting of
the Holders of shares of the class.

6.2

Subject to Article 6.3 below, the rights attached to any class of shares will be treated as varied (unless otherwise
expressly provided in the terms of issue of the shares concerned) by:

9

1955416363.11

6.2.1

the reduction of the capital paid up on that share class otherwise than by a lawful purchase or
redemption by the Company of its own shares; and

6.2.2

the allotment or issue of new shares:
6.2.2.1

ranking in priority for payment of a dividend or in respect of capital; or

6.2.2.2

which confer on its Holders voting rights that are more favourable than those conferred by
that class of shares.

6.3

The rights attached to any class of shares will not be treated as varied (unless otherwise expressly provided in the
terms conferring such rights) by the allotment or issue of new shares or securities convertible into shares of the
same class or shares ranking equally with, or junior to, that class of shares or by the lawful purchase or
redemption by the Company of its own shares of any class.

6.4

The rights attached to the Preferred Shares shall not be treated as varied by the allotment or issue of any Ordinary
Shares or securities convertible into Ordinary Shares.

7

CLASS MEETINGS

7.1

The provisions of these Articles as to general meetings apply to any separate general meeting of the Holders of
shares of a class (class meeting), subject to any necessary modifications in accordance with Company Legislation
or these Articles.

7.2

No Member, other than a Director, is entitled to notice of a class meeting or to attend it unless he is a Holder of
shares of the class concerned. No vote may be given except in respect of a share of that class.

7.3

For a class meeting in connection with the variation of rights attached to that class (variation of class rights
meeting):

7.4

7.3.1

the quorum (unless an adjourned meeting) is two persons present holding collectively at least one-third
in nominal value of the issued shares of that class;

7.3.2

the quorum for an adjourned meeting is one person present holding shares of that class;

7.3.3

a person present by proxy or proxies is treated as holding only the shares in respect of which those

7.3.4

DIVIDEND RIGHTS ATTACHING TO PREFERRED SHARES

8.1

Subject to the provisions of Article 69, the Preferred Dividend shall, without resolution of the Board or the
shareholders, accrue from day to day in respect of each Preferred Dividend Weekly Period. The record date for
the entitlement to the Preferred Dividend shall be determined by the Board in accordance with Article 77.

8.2

Subject to the provisions of Articles 69 and 8.6.1, the Board may, from time to time, by resolution of the Board,
acting in their sole discretion, vary the Preferred Dividend Rate for future Preferred Dividend Weekly Periods,
provided that:
8.2.1

the Preferred Dividend Rate shall not be more than 20 per cent. per annum of the Preferred Dividend
Reference Amount;

10

1955416363.11

8.2.2

the Preferred Dividend Rate shall not be lower than the sum of the Bank of England base rate on the
working day prior to such resolution plus 1 per cent. per annum of the Preferred Dividend Reference
Amount; and

8.2.3

the Preferred Dividend Rate shall not be decreased by more than 0.5% per annum of the Preferred
Dividend Reference Amount in aggregate during the course of any four-week period.

8.3

Subject to the provisions of Articles 69 and 8.4, the Preferred Dividend for a Preferred Dividend Weekly Period
shall be payable in cash weekly in arrears by no later than ten clear working days after the relevant Preferred
Dividend Weekly Period End Date (excluding the Preferred Dividend Weekly Period End Date and the date of
payment), or such earlier date as the Board may determine (the "Preferred Dividend Payment Date"). The
Preferred Dividend for a Preferred Dividend Weekly Period shall be paid to the Holders of Preferred Shares on
the register at the close of business on the record date for the entitlement to the Preferred Dividend (as
determined by the Board in accordance with Article 77), in respect of each Preferred Share then registered in
their names, and a Preferred Share issued in any week prior to the record date for the entitlement to the Preferred
Dividend will receive the full Preferred Dividend for that weekly period.

8.4

The Board may resolve at any time before a Preferred Dividend is paid that such Preferred Dividend shall not be
paid until such later time as the Board resolves and then shall be paid to the Holder of the relevant Preferred
Share at the time of payment, without assigning any reason and without incurring any liability to any shareholder.
No Preferred Dividend shall constitute a debt due from the Company to any shareholder. If the proposed
payment of any Preferred Dividend would breach Article 69, and the Board has not resolved that such Preferred
Dividend shall not be paid, the Board will be deemed to have resolved prior to that Preferred Dividend being paid
that such Preferred Dividend shall not be paid until such later time as the Board resolves and then shall be paid to
the Holder of the relevant Preferred Share at the time of payment.

8.5

Any Preferred Dividend that is not: (i) declared or deemed declared by the Board (as a result of the operation of
Article 69 or otherwise); or (ii) paid (whether as a result of the provisions of Article 8.4, Article 69 or otherwise),
in each case, on or before its Preferred Dividend Payment Date, will accumulate as a non-interest-bearing
cumulative arrears balance from its Preferred Dividend Payment Date. Such balance shall not compound, accrue
interest or otherwise increase other than through the accumulation of additional unpaid Preferred Dividends. The
provisions of this Article 8.5 shall not apply to any Preferred Dividend in respect of which the relevant Holder of
Preferred Shares has waived the right to receive by giving notice to the Company.

8.6

If any Preferred Dividend is not paid in full on or before its Preferred Dividend Payment Date (excluding any
Preferred Dividend in respect of which the relevant Holder of Preferred Shares has waived the right to receive by
giving notice to the Company), then for so long as any part of it remains unpaid:
8.6.1

the Board may not decrease the Preferred Dividend Rate for any future Preferred Dividend Weekly
Period;

8.6.2

the Company may not pay dividends or make any other distribution on the Preferred Shares in relation
to any Preferred Dividend Weekly Period falling after the relevant Preferred Dividend Payment Date on
which payment was not made;

8.6.3

the Company may not declare or pay dividends or make any other distribution on any shares ranking
pari passu with the Preferred Shares, unless such dividend or distribution is also declared, paid or made
pari passu to Holders of Preferred Shares;

8.6.4

the Company may not declare or pay dividends or make any other distribution on the Ordinary Shares
or on any other share capital ranking junior to the Preferred Shares; and

confirmation or report, the Auditor may rely on advice or information from any source that it considers
appropriate.

authorising resolution. The Board may exclude from the offer any Holders where the Board considers that the

his right to make an election under a scrip dividend offer, or the non-receipt of any such notice (even if the
